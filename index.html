A seat at our table

A little place with a lot of heart.

Olive Cup started with a simple idea: the neighbourhood deserves a place that feels like yours. Somewhere to slow down, say hello, and enjoy something made with care.

We pour coffee from nearby roasters, bake in small batches, and save you a seat whenever you need one. Come as you are; we'll put the kettle on.

“The best part of the day is the part we share.”
